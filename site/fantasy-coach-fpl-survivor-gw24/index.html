We may be in the middle of week with European Cup games, but that doesn't prevent me to give you an update on the Fantasy Coach's FPL Survivor following the end of GW24.

With gameweeks passing by, the number of managers left in the competition is dwindling (as it should in a survivor). Moving to the next match week forces managers to make less and less mistakes.

To survive GW24, managers had to score a minimum of 43pts. 10 didn't make it. 170 will battle it out in DGW25 where 4 teams have a double bill. The deadline for this new gameweek is Saturday 17th February at 12h CET (11am GMT).

The 4 teams with a double gameweek are: Brentford (LIV,mci), Liverpool (bre,LUT), Luton (MUN,liv) et Manchester City (CHE,BRE).
