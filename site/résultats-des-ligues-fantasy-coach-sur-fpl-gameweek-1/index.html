  • Ligue FPL Challenge (code: 5n6fr5)

Cette saison le FPL Challenge va se dérouler par succession d’évènement. Le premier évènement et donc le 5-a-side. Le premier challenge de cet évènement demandait de constituer une équipe de 5 joueur avec un budget illimité.

Sur cette 1ere gameweek c’est Mahmoud Mohamed qui le remporte avec 66pts. Il prend la tête de l’évènement 5-a-side et du classement de la saison.

Pour la gameweek 2 le challenge sera “All out attack”. Il vous faudra constituer une équipe avec 1 gardien et 4 attaquants (FWD). Pour corser le tout le budget n’est que de 35M. Les buts marqués par vos joueurs compteront double: 20pts si c’est votre gardien qui marque et 8pts pour vos attaquants.

Bonne semaine et rappeler vous que ce n’est que le début du championnat. Il y a encore plein de point à prendre. Pas de panique si votre score de la GW1 vous a déçu.

In this article, after each round of play (gameweek), I will try to give you an update on the different competitions Fantasy Coach offers you.

I'm starting this series following the first matchday of the Premier League. Except Tottenham who were held to a draw by Leicester, and Chelsea who fell against City, all the big guns won. Salah, the king of FPL, posted the highest score with 14 pts. Saka, Havertz and Welbeck are not far behind with 12pts.

Fantasy Coach’s League's & Cups

  • The Classic League (code: gpojm8):

The top 10 managers are within 9pts of each other’s. It’s already a fair gap after just 1 gameweek. Of course it still is early days and things will change. One thing to mention is that all these scores were done without using a chip.

Feyi Ajinni is the first leader of our league. With Salah captain, Saka, Havertz and Porro he already had a good base. Arthur, last season's champion, also made a good start since he is tied in 6th place. He will be in the mix once again for the final victory.

  • The Fantasy-coach.fr’s Cup (code: gpojm8):

This competition will not begin until the last 10 gameweeks of the season. But, thanks the larger number of mangers of our league, we will have one extra round of matches compared to last season. The Fantsy-coach.fr’s Cup will start in GW28.

  • Fantasy Coach’s FPL Survivor (code: gpojm8)

We were waiting GW1 deadline to determine how Survivor would pan out for this season. With 520 managers involved, here is the program.

Gameweek 1 to 10, a minimum of 8 managers will be eliminated per gameweek;

Gameweek 11 to 20, a minimum of 12 managers will be eliminated per gameweek;

Gameweek 21 to 25, a minimum of 17 managers will be eliminated per gameweek;

Gameweek 26 to 30, a minimum of 22 managers will be eliminated per gameweek;

At the end of GW30, there should be around 135 managers left. From gameweek 31 to 33, the group will be further trimmed to keep exactly 60 managers before the start of GW34.

30 managers will be eliminated on GW34

15 managers will be eliminated on GW35.

The 4 best scores of GW36 will qualify for the final. The final four will take place in GW37.

For GW1 the safety score was 36 points. 8 managers had lower scores. They therefore exit the competition.

Michael Akins, last season's champion, went through without breaking a sweat with 67pts.

  • FPL Challenge League (code: 5n6fr5)

This season the FPL Challenge is split in different event. For the first four gameweeks, the event is called 5-a-side.

The first challenge of this event required to build a team of 5 with unlimited budget. This challenge was won by Mahmoud Mohamed with 66pts. He takes the lead in the 5-a-side event. He also leads the season ranking.

For gameweek 2 the challenge will be “All out attack”. You will need to select a team with 1 goalkeeper (GK) and 4 forwards (FWD) with a 35M budget.

The goals scored by your players will count double: 20pts each if your goalkeeper nets one, 8pts each if it is your forwards.

Have a good week and remember that this is only the beginning of the game. There are still plenty of points to take. Don't panic if your GW1 score was not up to your expectations.
