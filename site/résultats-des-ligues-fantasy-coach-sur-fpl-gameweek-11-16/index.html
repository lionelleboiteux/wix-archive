It's been a while since I last updated you on the progress of the FPL competitions of fantasy-coach.fr.

With the Winter Cup semi-finals taking place this weekend, it's interesting to see who might win the first trophy of the season.

Let's dive into the details and see what's happened in the various leagues and cups over the last two months.

Fantasy Coach’s League's & Cups

  • The Classic League (code: da3dv8):

Hamza Riffi has a firm grip of the lead in the classic league. He's maintaining a ten-point lead over the rest of the pack, no matter what. He is in front of two managers who have given the same name to their respective teams. They seem to like the famous chocolate bar from the Ferrero brand. Lacoste - is 10 points behind Hamza. The third member of the podium is Subhan Yaqub.

The points difference within the top 10 continues to grow, a testament to Hamza's strength. He's 56 points ahead of Nicolas Pluchon, in 10th place.

n Douglas, the 2025 champion, continues to hold a place in the top 10. Since Matchday 10, he's climbed three places to reach 4th, 14 points off the podium.

Asare Daniel, winner of the Winter Cup and the Classic Cup in 2024-2025, has finally found his form after a very difficult start to the season. He climbed 172 places in six rounds of game, returning to a more respectable 244th place.

Wan Fayshal, the Survivor 2025 winner, progressed slightly less dramatically than Asare, gaining 48 places to reach 135th in the Classic League.

  • Fantasy Coach’s FPL Survivor (code: da3dv8)

Since GW11, 12 managers have been eliminated each round. In 6 rounds, we have therefore lost 72 managers. There are now 326 managers remaining in the Survivor 2026 competition.

The minimum scores required in GW11 to GW16 can be found at the end of this paragraph.

Florian Paris is performing exceptionally well to stay in the competition. He has narrowly escaped elimination several times, but he has done enough to remain after 16 rounds. He is currently 489th in the Classic League, 8 places above the bottom of the standings.

In the Head-to-Head league, Eduardo Alvizurez is in the lead. Out of the six games played, he has only lost one. He has a three-point lead over Ovidiu Lucian, who has become his new closest rival. I'm clinging to the last step of the podium despite a nightmarish period with very low scores.

  • FPL Challenge League (code: ojmldm)

Over six gameweeks, two events, each with three challenges, took place.

Between GW11 and GW13, the "Football Manager" event was held. It was won by Sarawut Assawachaichit, finishing 10 points ahead of Matt Aldinho. The challenges and their winners are as follows:

"Change the Game," won by Jevante Lewis in GW11;

"In Possession," won by Matty Rowett in GW12;

"Out of Possession," won by O. Keith Ajayi in GW13.

Between GW14 and GW16, the "Cut-Price Gems" event took place. The victory in this event was shared between Arafat Ibnul Bashar and Nicolas Pluchon. The challenges and their winners are as follows:

"Value Creators," won by Arafat Ibnul Bashar in GW14;

"Big Save" was won by O. Keith Ajayi in GW15;

"Striker Deal" was won by MisterVN- in GW16.

In the overall season standings, Jackie Winchole has regained the lead, but the gap with second place is far from huge. Second place is held by Sarawut, the winner of the "Football Manager" event. The final spot on the podium goes to Matt Aldinho.

GW17 marks the start of a new FPL Challenge event called "The Festive Fixtures." It will run until GW20. The first challenge is called "Home for the Holidays." You will have to select 6 players with an unlimited budget. As the name suggests, players who have home games this weekend will have their points doubled. Choose your Bournemouth and Manchester City players wisely.

Good luck for GW17 and see you after Christmas for the Boxing Day gameweek.
