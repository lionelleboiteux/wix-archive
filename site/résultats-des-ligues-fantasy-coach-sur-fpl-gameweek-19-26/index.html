  • Fantasy Coach’s Winter Cup (code: da3dv8)

During GW19, the final gameweek of 2025, the Winter Cup final took place. It pitted the overwhelming favorite, Julien Cazenave, who had already won the classic Cup in 2024, against Kisakye Mack. In addition to being formidable in head-to-head matches, Julien was also high in the overall fantasy-coach league rankings. He even made it into the top 5 in GW26. Congratulations to Kisakye for this flawless performance.

  • H2H League

In the Head-to-Head league, without any extraordinary feats, Ovidiu Lucian has taken the lead, despite three losses in his last eight games. Morgan RF is now in second place, with Umar Kamsulum completing the podium.

The top 8 playoff spots remain within six points. Khurshid Khakimjanov is the last remaining playoff contender and holds a slim three-point advantage over the chasing pack.

"Double Celebration," won by Haidar Abdulaziz in GW19;

"Bragging Rights" was won by Memerang Laut Bipolar in GW25;

"Classic Rivals" was won by Sarawut Assawachaichit in GW26.

In the overall season standings, Olukayode Olotu is in first place, but the battle with Jevante Lewis is intense, with only 7 points separating them. Sarawut Assawachaichit completes the podium.

Jackie Winchole, who led the FPL Challenge league for a long time, is currently in 4th place.

Arafat Ibnul Bashar, who won the "Cut-Price Gems" event, is ranked 11th.

Nicolas Pluchon, who won the "Archetype" event, is 5th in the overall standings.

The third challenge of the "Rivalries" event, "Old Foes," will take place in GW27. You will have to select 6 players with an unlimited budget. Players from Nottingham Forest and Liverpool will have their points doubled. You can select a maximum of 3 players from each of these two clubs.
