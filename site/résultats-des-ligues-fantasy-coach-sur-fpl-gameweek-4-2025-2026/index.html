Gameweek 4 of the Fantasy Premier League (FPL) marked the return of the championship following the international break. The teams competing in the Champions League are starting their marathons and will start rotations in their starting XI... or not (I'm looking at you, Mr. Arteta).

Here is the update on the fantasy-coach.fr competitions following Matchday 4 of the Premier League.

Fantasy Coach’s League's & Cups

  • The Classic League (code: da3dv8):

Another round of play is done, and we have a new leader. It's the 4th in 4 rounds. A sign that the early season is competitive at Fantasy Coach. Another Hamza succeeds Hamza Abda, who drops to second in the standings. Hamza Riffi overtakes him. The podium is completed by GW2 leader Mouhaned Torban. It's worth noting that Hamza Riffi is no stranger to good starts. He was top of the league in GW8 last season. He eventually finished 50th.

The point difference in the top 10 is stable, increasing by only 1 point. Hamza is ahead of 10th place Kevin Rose by 28 points.

n. Douglas, the 2025 champion, gains 3 places and already reaches the top 10. He is 9th in the overall standings. The Scot isn't going to give up his crown easily.

Asare Daniel, winner of both cups in 2025, continues to struggle. He drops 48 places to 402nd. A real setback! He'll have to recover by GW9, otherwise he might not even be able to defend his Winter Cup crown.

Wan Fayshal, the winner of Survivor 2025, on the other hand, has made a meteoric climb up. He ranks 122nd, a huge 130 places better than in GW3.

  • Fantasy Coach’s FPL Survivor (code: da3dv8)

Another reminder for those who missed the first article of the season, here is how the Survivor 3 eliminations will take place.

GW 1 to 10, a minimum of 9 managers will be eliminated per gameweek;

GW 11 to 20, 13 managers will be eliminated per gameweek;

GW 21 to 25, 14 managers will be eliminated per gameweek;

GW 26 to 30, 19 managers will be eliminated per gameweek;

At the end of GW30, there should be around 110 managers remaining.

GW 31 to 33, the eliminations will allow exactly 50 managers to remain before the start of GW34.

25 managers will be eliminated in GW34.

13 managers will be eliminated in GW35.

The four best scores from G 36 will qualify for the final, which will take place in GW37.

For your information : starting from GW2, if there are tied managers for the last surviving spot, it will be the Overall Points that will be the decider, then it will be the lowest number of transfers.

As expected, nine managers were eliminated after GW4. To survive and reach GW5, the minimum score to achieve was 41 points with an OP of 159 points. There are 455 managers left in the race.

At the start of the season, survival is easier when you don't take too many hits with your transfers.

The best score of the gameweek was achieved by Yusuph Ola with 98 points.

Wan Fayshal, the winner of Survivor 2, easily made it through this fourth round with 85 points.

GW4 marked the start of the second event of the season: "Beat The Icon." It began with the "Classic Rivalry" challenge, where Manchester City and Manchester United players had their points doubled.
