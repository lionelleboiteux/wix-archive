I will try to be quick for this update of the Fantasy Coach FPL competitions. There is not much time before GW14 begins.

City's hopes of a 5th title in a row were dented this weekend in Liverpool. We can never write off the Sky Blues from the title race because they rarely lose matches in the 2nd half of the season. But Pep and his players are really not in a good place at the moment.

Arsenal will be there to take advantage of any slip up of the Reds. If Liverpool is irresistible, the Gunners are impressive since the return of Odegaard.

Don’t forget the GW14 deadline which is this Tuesday at 7 p.m. CET (6 p.m. GMT).

The Classic League (code: gpojm8):

The Salah/Palmer/Saka trio is the current magic combination. We'll have to find a way to combine the 2 Mancunians from United Rashford and Bruno Fernandes with the Magic Three to spend a nice festive period. As I wrote last weekend, we absolutely must nail the captain each gameweek. This is where the differences will be made.

Philippe Demaesneer had a second poor performance in a row and it pushed him out of the top 10. Julien Cazenave is rewarded for his consistency since the start of the season. He takes the lead in our league. We also see the return to grace of Hamza Riffi who takes 2nd place just 1 point behind Julien. The brand-new podium is completed by Abdelhadi Aziz.

The average performances of the 3 former leaders narrows the point gaps in the top 10. There is now only a 22-points difference in the classic league’s top 10.

Arthur Simmonet, 2021, 2022 and 2023 champion, is still stuck beyond the 300th place. He scored a healthy 74pts which helped him rise 36 places. He is now 347th. At least the slump has stopped for the moment.

Julien Cazenave, the winner of the 2024 Cup, as we saw above is the new leader of the league thanks to his 3 places gained.

Sean Gardener, the winner of the 2023 Winter Cup, is slowly progressing with 21 places gained and an overall ranking of 432nd.

Michael Akins, the winner of Survivor 2024, continues and gains 18 places in GW13. He is now 61st.

Fantasy Coach’s FPL Survivor (code: gpojm8)

To survive GW13 and move on to GW14, you needed to avoid poor choices. The 14 managers eliminated in GW13 didn’t make the surviving score of 40pts with 754pts over the season. A relatively high minimum score which shows a good average level of managers in the league.

Michael Akins, the 2024 champion, and his 84pts didn't even break a sweat in GW13. Michael is making a very good defense of his title so far.

The best score of the day is achieved by Jonas Karera with 105pts. He gets in the top 25 of the classic league with this total.

The manager with the worst overall ranking still in the race in Survivor continues to be Michael Ogunyemi. He goes up 4 places to rank 536th.

The league point average for GW12 is 65pts.

Fantasy Coach's Winter Cup (code: gpojm8)

Before a break in GW14, the Winter Cup saw its Round 4 played in GW13.

The match between Anuoluwapo Michael Oluwaseyi and Allen Kardic ended in a draw. Allan goes through to the round of 16 thanks to a greater number of goals scored by his team. 4 against 2 for Anuoluwapo.

I am the only former Fantasy Coach winner still in the race. In GW15 I will be facing Eric Teh. Amin Kammoun was beaten by Julius Likonstane.

For the round of 16, half of the qualified are ranked in the top 100, 7 are ranked between the 100th and the 200th place, and 1 ranked between the 200th and the 300th place. After 4 rounds the triming is over. Things are getting serious. The managers with the best overall standings can now be considered favorites for victory in GW19. They are Eric Teh, Mordyn Doubell and Eric Margery.

The match with the biggest rank difference will be between Moldavine Mansa and Julius Likonstane who are 112 places apart. The most even match will be between Eric Margery and Paul MacAffrey who are separated by only 43 places.

H2H League

For once it's a status quo at the top of the H2H league. The podium is unchanged. Faloye Oluwaseyi and Dmitry Gor have won their matches and continue to be tied at the top. Faloye is still ahead thanks to his best overall score of the season. Tom Nettlelton remains 3rd.

The victory with the biggest point difference (47) is the work of our Racoonn. He consoles himself of his loss last week in the Winter Cup with a thrashing that takes him to the 11th place, 8 points behind the 2 leaders.

FPL Challenge League (code 5n6fr5)

As writen last week, in the challenge "Salah or Haaland?" the answer was Salah. You had to select 5 players and those from Liverpool and Manchester City saw their points doubled. There was no point in taking assets from the Citizens as they maxed on 2 pts.

The challenge was won by Jackie Winchole who beat Kareem Farah by 2pts. Sebastian Przybylowski finished 3rd and showed his total domination of this event.

He is firmly in the lead for the "Goal Diggers" event. He leads Felix Ogunniyi in 2nd place, and Dawid Malek in 3rd place.

Sebastian domination over the last gameweeks finally takes to the helm in the Fantasy Coach FPL Challenge league for the season. Anp S drops to 2nd place. Aled Wilcox remains 3rd. Jesukorede Adelowokan is starting to let go and he falls to the 25th place. I recover slightly by gaining 6 places and I reach the 29th place.

The challenge of the GW14 is "Red Hot Deal". With a budget of 75M you need to select 11 players, that is to say an entire team. For this round of play, the players of Manchester United (the Red Devils) will see their points doubled. As a reminder, the players of Amorim will face the steamroller Arsenal and the match will be at the Emirates. So be measured when you select your ManU players. There’s little chance for a Mancunian goal fest.

I wanted to be concise. I think I managed to be as long as usual. I doubt that I will be able to write an article after the GW14 but the leagues, cups and other competitions will be kept up to date. Good luck to all for the gameweek 14. The GW15 arrives right after and will kick off with the last Merseyside derby at Goodison Park.

FPL Challenge League (code 5n6fr5)

Comme indiqué la semaine dernière, dans le challenge « Salah or Haaland ? » il fallait répondre Salah. Il fallait donc sélectionner 5 joueurs et ceux de Liverpool et de Manchester City voyaient leurs points doubler. Il est toujours en tête de l’évènement « Goal Diggers ». Il devance Felix Ogunniyi  en 2e place, et Dawid Malek en 3e place.

Jesukorede Adelowokan commence à lâcher et il se retrouve à la 25e place. Je me reprends légèrement en gagnant 6 places et j’atteinds la 29e place.

Pour ce tour de jeu, les joueurs de Manchester United (les Red Devils) verront leurs points doublés. Pour rappel, les joueurs d’Amorim seront face au rouleau compresseur Arsenal et le match sera à l’Emirates. Donc soyez mesuré lors de votre sélection. Les buts Mancuniens ne seront pas nombreux.
