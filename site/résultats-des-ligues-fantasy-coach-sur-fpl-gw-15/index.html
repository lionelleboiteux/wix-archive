We get a short break in the frenzy calendar of this final month of the year in the Premier League. It was just enough time for the Fantasy Premier League to unveil what the Mystery Chip was going to be. It is called "Assistant Manager". It will allow you to recruit one of the 20 PL managers, on your budget, with prices from 0.5M to 1.5M. This chip will be available from GW24 and will be valid for 3 gameweeks after its activation.

Because of storm Darragh, there was no Merseyside Derby in GW15. Salah and co. will have (at least) one double gameweek in the future as a result of this postponement. And if the little Pharaoh keeps up his current form, he will be a good candidate for Triple Captain.

Only Chelsea took advantage of the Reds' forced holidays. This time the Spurs did not finish with 9 players, but still the were roasted upside down by the Blues. Terrible.

The deadline for GW16 is this Saturday, December 14th, 2024 at 2:30pm CET (1:30pm GMT).

The Classic League (code: gpojm8):

The other hero, apart from Darragh, during this GW15 was Cole Palmer. The young Englishman gave my sorry Spurs a hard time. Those who gave him the armband (or Salah's vice-captaincy) are the winners of this gameweek as Saka blanked at Craven Cottage and Haaland only scored one goal.

Julien Cazenave takes back the lead in our league but the point gaps are hard to widen. His throne is very unstable. Abdelhadi Aziz is the new runner-up. Axel Dancette, who was a long-term tenant of the top 10 last season, returns to the forefront and completes the podium.

The point gap in the top 10 is getting a little boost but it is still quite tight. We are still far from the outright domination that Arthur imposed on us in previous seasons. There is a 30-point gap between 1st and 10th.

Arthur Simmonet, the 2021, 2022 and 2023 champion, still cannot get his team to be consistent. He loses ground again and finds himself in 360th place, 9 places less than in GW14.

Julien Cazenave, the winner of the 2024 Cup, as we saw above, is the leader and is 9 points clear from the 2nd place.

Sean Gardener, the winner of the 2023 Winter Cup, takes a big dive with 47 places lost. He is now 486th.

Michael Akins, the winner of Survivor 2024, also loses ground with 11 places less. He dropped in 2 gameweeks from 61st to 86th.

Fantasy Coach’s FPL Survivor (code: gpojm8)

For the last 3 gameweeks of the Fantasy Coach’s Survivor, we have moved to another level of difficulty. It is dangerous to score less than 30pts. The minimum score to achieve during this GW15 was 30pts with an overall score for the season of 840pts.

14 managers left again the competition, including Sean Gardener. 371 will see GW16.

Michael Akins, the 2024 champion, passes this round of game relatively safe. His score of 47pts (51, -4) still leaves him far from the elimination zone.

The best score of the gameweek is achieved by Ci Amin with only 88pts.

With 13 places gained, Michael Ogunyemi is no longer the last of the survivors. He leaves the title of manager with the worst general standing still in the race to Kwadwo Stun who is 528th.

The league point average for GW15 is 53.2pts.

Fantasy Coach's Winter Cup (code: gpojm8)

The Winter Cup get closer and closer to the final stage. There are only 2 rounds of play left before the final.

I lose my Round of 16 match to Eric Teh. Rayan Aït Nouri's assist was the fatal blow for my team.

Of the 8 managers qualified for the quarter-finals of the Fantasy Coach's Winter Cup, 7 are in the top 100, the last one is ranked between 100th and 200th place. It's no joke anymore. The 3 members of the current top 10 are objectively the solid favorites. They are Eric Teh, Mordyn Doubell and Pierre Monin.

The match with the biggest rank difference will be between Pierre and Lyndon Fernandes who are 176 places apart. The most even match will be between Mordyn Doubell and Paul MacAffrey who are separated by only 39 places.

The fixture of the quarter finals of the Winter Cup are as follow:

For the right part of the table:

Pierre Monin (10th) v. Lyndon Fernandes (186th)

H2H League

It’s the great status quo in the H2H league. For the moment, none of the top 5 is messing around. The podium is unchanged. Faloye Oluwasey is leading Dmitry Gor and Tom Nettlelton in that order.

The victory with the largest point difference (34) is the work of Samuel Barden. This victory brings him to 25th place.

FPL Challenge League (code 5n6fr5)

GW15 challenge was called "Big Spenders". You had to select 11 players with a budget of 100M. Players who had an FPL price higher than 9M had their points doubled. Cole Palmer haul allowed to get huge scores, even more than in the classic version of the game. Zubail Kaweesi wins this 15th challenge of the season. He is ahead of Zakaria El Manyani, 2nd, and Michael De Salazar, 3rd.

Sebastian Przybylowski drops a few points, but he is in pole position and in a good position to win the "Goal Diggers" event. Michael de Salazar, Zakaria El Manyani and Aled Wilcox are the only ones who could get in Sebastian’s way of a 2nd victory in a row.

Sebastian and Aled continue their mano a mano at the top of the FPL Challenge league on the season. Philippe Moreira-Bessa returns to the top three. Jesukorede Adelowokan stabilizes his ranking. He takes 35th place. For my part, I am getting closer to the top 20, in 21st place of the league.

The GW16 challenge is called "Bargain". With a budget of 100M you need select 11 players. This time, it will be the players whose FPL price is less than 6M who get their points doubled.

Good luck to everyone for GW16. Don't be surprised by the deadline which is later than usual. You can party Friday night and sleep in though.

FPL Challenge League (code 5n6fr5)

Le challenge de la GW15 s’appelait « Big Spenders. Il fallait sélectionner 11 joueurs avec un budget de 100M. Les joueurs qui avaient un prix FPL supérieur à 9M voyaient leurs points doublés. Cole Palmer a permis de faire d’énormes scores, encore plus que dans la version classique du jeu. Zubail Kaweesi remporte ce 15e challenge. Il devance Zakaria El Manyani, 2e, et Michael De Salazar, 3e. 

Sebastian Przybylowski lâche quelques points, mais il est en pole position et en bonne position pour remporter l’évènement « Goal Diggers ». Michael de Salazar, Zakaria El Manyani et Aled Wilcox sont les seuls qui pourraient priver Sebastian d’une 2e victoire d’affilée.

Sebastian et Aled continuent leur mano à mano en tête de la ligue FPL Challenge sur la saison. Philippe Moreira-Bessa revient dans le trio de tête. Jesukorede Adelowokan stabilise son classement. Il prend la 35e place. De mon côté je me rapproche du top 20 en occupant la 21e place de la ligue.

Bonne chance à tous pour la GW16. Ne vous faites pas surprendre par la deadline qui est plus tard que d’habitude. Vous pouvez faire la fête Vendredi soir et faire la grasse mat’.
