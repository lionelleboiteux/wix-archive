There are only nine matches left for Premier League teams before the end of the season. Nine gameweeks for FPL managers to navigate. If you're more than 90 points behind the leader of your mini-league, your hopes for victory are slim, much like those of the 19 teams behind Liverpool. Although, the Reds have stalled abruptly and will have to be very serious to maintain their lead over Arsenal. The Gunners will soon see Saka back. This should help their goalscoring prowess.

We also arrive at the season of blank and double gameweeks (BGW/DGW). After GW29, which was the first blank of the season with four teams without matches, blanks and doubles will follow one after the other. In GW31, the first double gameweek will involve Newcastle (MUN, CRY) and Crystal Palace (mci, new). Two gameweeks later, the teams qualified for the FA Cup quarterfinals will have DGW. But because of the clash between GW34 and the FA Cup quarter finals, the same teams (and the teams they play against) will blank.

GW37 clashes with the FA Cup semifinals. So the teams playing at Wembley will blank. Their outstanding game will be scheduled either in GW35 (low probability) and/or in GW36 (high probability).

Anyway, to know when matches happen or not, stay tuned to Raccoon player picks.

For those who have their "Assistant Manager" chip active or are thinking of activating it in GW30, here are the managers who can earn you bonus points in the event of a win (+5 points) or a draw (+3 points): Marco Silva vs. Arsenal; Ruben Amorim vs. Nottingham Forest; Kieran McKenna vs. Bournemouth; Ruud Van Nistelrooy vs. Manchester City; Thomas Frank vs. Newcastle; Ivan Juric vs. Crystal Palace; David Moyes vs. Liverpool; and Ange Postecoglou vs. Chelsea.

In the meantime, I'll give you an update on the Fantasy Coach leagues and cups in FPL.

The Classic League (code: gpojm8):

Pierre Monin, who reached the top of our classic league thanks, in part, to optimal use of the "Assistant Manager" chip from GW24 to GW26, held on until GW28. He struggled over the last 2 gameweeks and dropped to 3rd at the end of GW29. We consequently have a new leader in Yague Diaw. He has been slightly more consistent and he took advantage of the poor form of the ones in front of him. He gained  four places and the throne. Just three points behind stands Julien Cazenave, who keeps his second place.

The points gap in the top 10 has dropped below 30 points. It's now only 27 points after climbing to a maximum of 77 points in GW23. The chips and the managers' expertise in navigating blanks and doubles will see the standings constantly changing between now and the end of the season.

Arthur Simmonet, the 2021, 2022, and 2024 champion, is still struggling to break into the league's top 300. Despite a gain of 7 places since GW26, he is 328th.

Julien Cazenave, the 2024 Cup winner, remains in 2nd place. He failed to capitalize on Pierre Monin's less than average performances and he was even overtaken by Yague.

Asare Daniel, the 2024 Winter Cup winner, more than halved his ranking in three gameweeks. He gained 36 places to reach 32nd place.

Michael Akins, the 2024 Survivor winner, on the other hand, more than doubled his ranking. He dropped 16 places to reach 30th place in the league.

Fantasy Coach’s FPL Cup (code: gpojm8)

The qualifying round of the Fantasy Coach Cup took place in GW28. With 552 managers registered in the league, the players needed to be among the 472 best scores of GW28 to qualify directly for Round 1 played in GW30. FPL for the qualifying round does not take into account the hits. Players who scored less than 47pts in GW28 had to play a Preliminary Round in GW29.

A few well-known managers from previous seasons got caught and had to play. Romain Ferron, in addition to being eliminated in Survivor in GW28, was knocked out of the Cup by John Terry in GW29. Antoine Gadala goes through, beating Muhammad Saitama. He will face Joao Coiba in Round 1. Stephan Yodsak lost to Jackie Winchole. Gio Alarcon (a multiple top 10K finisher), also went out from the Survivor in GW28. He narrowly advanced to Round 1, defeating Eddie Fondo by just 1 point. It was even closer for Pierre Mouton, who goes through on virtual coin toss. Damilola Damini is the culprit on that occasion. Finally, Matthieu Moreira-Bessa, who performs well in the FPL challenge, will not see Round 1 after his defeat against Lone Ranger.
